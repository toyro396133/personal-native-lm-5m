# Structural trajectory: 150M -> 170M -> 200M

This document consolidates the three v3 structural checkpoint audits.

Primary principle: language quality is a health/control metric, not the primary
SELF research score.

## Stable conclusions across the trajectory

1. **CORE does not require explicit SELF.**
   Baseline preserves strong CORE organization throughout.

2. **SELF direction is functionally special.**
   Magnitude-matched radial perturbations consistently cause much larger hidden
   representation effects than random/orthogonal perturbations at the same
   displacement.

3. **SELF strength != learned SELF reference quality.**
   Architectures such as diff_anchor and self_v1 can have very strong SELF
   effects while learned GOAL/FOCUS reference semantics remain negative.

4. **CORE robustness is an independent axis.**
   Large SELF effects can coexist with robust CORE (self_v1) or with severe
   entanglement (diff_anchor).

5. **FOCUS is a stronger challenge dimension than GOAL.**
   GOAL generally preserves CORE classification, while FOCUS produces
   intermediate-layer degradation in several architectures.

6. **Internal organization is richer than final output geometry.**
   GOAL/FOCUS relation margins often peak in middle layers and are compressed
   by the final representation.

7. **Development is non-monotonic.**
   150->170->200 cannot be summarized as simple scalar strengthening.
