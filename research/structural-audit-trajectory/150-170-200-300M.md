# Final structural trajectory: 150M -> 170M -> 200M -> 300M

This document closes the v0.18 seven-arm continuation from 100M to 300M.

Primary principle: language quality is a health/control metric, not the main
SELF research score.

## Stable conclusions across the full trajectory

1. **CORE does not require explicit SELF.** Baseline carries strong CORE and
   relation geometry throughout.
2. **The learned SELF direction is functionally special.** Magnitude-matched
   radial perturbations repeatedly produce much larger effects than
   random/orthogonal directions.
3. **SELF strength, learned-reference quality and CORE robustness are distinct
   axes.**
4. **The projected family gives the cleanest persistent learned CORE reference.**
5. **FOCUS remains a stronger challenge dimension than GOAL.**
6. **Internal layers carry richer relational structure than final hidden state.**
7. **Development is strongly non-monotonic.** There is no single smooth SELF
   maturation curve.
8. **Longer training can increase entanglement rather than improve structure.**
   diff_only at 300M is the clearest example.

## Final research-role map

### projected_diff
Best persistent learned SELF coordinate for CORE.

At 300M:
- learned CORE +0.00953
- learned FOCUS +0.00881
- final CORE survives radial-2x
- learned GOAL remains positive in layers 1–4 and becomes negative only later

This is the cleanest model for studying SELF-as-reference-coordinate.

### projected_diff_slow
Best long-run balanced separation profile.

Across all four audited milestones, final CORE survives radial-2x. It also
maintains positive learned CORE and FOCUS specificity, although final GOAL
specificity reverses by 300M after being positive at earlier milestones.

This is the cleanest model for studying stable dual-anchor organization under a
slow reference schedule.

### diff_only
A learned-reference architecture that becomes too entangled late.

Its radial-2x CORE trajectory:
0.833 -> 0.667 -> 1.000 -> **0.333**

This is direct evidence that more training can reorganize SELF/CORE coupling in
a harmful direction.

### diff_anchor
The strongest negative control for "SELF strength = success".

The SELF direction is extremely influential, but GOAL/FOCUS learned-reference
polarity remains wrong and CORE is strongly dependent on SELF perturbation.

### self_v1
A strong and reusable modulation direction with poor learned downstream
reference semantics.

### self_v1_slow
A distinct late phenotype: strong raw FOCUS relation, weak CORE reference and
partial radial-2x CORE fragility.

### baseline
Necessary control proving that CORE and relation geometry exist before explicit
SELF is introduced.

## Main conclusion

The completed 100M->300M experiment no longer supports a simple serial story
such as:

`SELF -> CORE -> GOAL -> FOCUS`

The better model is:

`SELF <-> CORE`

with GOAL and FOCUS dynamically organized relative to those anchors across
depth and training time.

The key observation is not that one variant wins every metric. It is that
projected architectures most consistently preserve a distinct SELF/CORE
reference structure while non-projected architectures often turn SELF strength
into entanglement.

## What 300M did not prove

- semantic selfhood;
- a literal causal SELF x CORE -> GOAL circuit;
- direct causal status of GOAL or FOCUS;
- multi-seed generalization;
- that final hidden-state geometry is the true computational endpoint.
